What it does

Spots emergencies

Questions shop owners ask

Does it sound like a robot?

It speaks in a natural, friendly voice and holds a normal conversation. Call the demo and judge for yourself. It also tells callers up front that it's an AI assistant.

What if the caller wants a real person?

It takes a detailed message with their name, number, and the issue, and lets them know someone from your shop will call them back.

What about emergencies?

It recognizes urgent situations like gas smells, flooding, or no heat in cold weather, gives basic safety guidance, and marks the message as urgent so you can follow up quickly. For a gas leak it tells callers to get out and call the gas utility or 911.

How do I keep my number?

You keep it. Your existing business line is simply forwarded to the receptionist. We help you set up forwarding, and you can turn it off anytime.

What does it cost?

It's $29/month for 100 minutes (founding price for the first 5 shops), with extra minutes at $0.35.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. No contract.
